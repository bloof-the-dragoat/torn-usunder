# Part 1: Burden

Bloof is surviving, not thriving.

Work. College. Training. A small home, and the person who made it one. Every part of that life has rules: keep your head down, stay useful, stay unnoticed, and never let anyone ask about the years you cannot remember.

It almost works.

But the past does not stay buried because someone wishes it would. And it has already begun to move.
